SAMPLE INTERNAL POLICY — QA and Release Management
This is an illustrative company template, not a statement of legal or regulatory requirements.

Quality checks
- Define acceptance criteria before implementation and keep test evidence with the work item or release.
- Run relevant automated tests and document any known failures or approved exceptions.
- Test changes in a non-production environment before production deployment.
- Do not use real customer or employee data in tests unless specifically approved and protected.

Change and release controls
- Every production change has an accountable owner, peer review, test evidence, and an authorized release approval.
- Record the release version, deployment time, outcome, and rollback plan.
- Confirm service health after deployment. If a release causes material impact, follow the incident process and record corrective actions.
- Emergency changes must be documented and reviewed after stabilization.

Questions about this sample
The company owner must adapt this template to actual systems, contracts, and applicable law before adopting it.
