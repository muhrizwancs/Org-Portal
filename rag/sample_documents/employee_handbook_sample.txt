SAMPLE EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK
This is a fictional, illustrative company template. It does not set actual employment terms or legal requirements.

Working together
- Treat colleagues and customers with respect, communicate clearly, and raise workplace concerns through the appropriate manager or HR contact.
- Keep company information confidential and use approved company accounts, devices, and storage for work.
- Follow the security, privacy, safety, and conduct policies that apply to your role.

Time away and support
Use the portal's Requests page to submit leave, training, equipment, or access requests when the feature is available. The manager or administrator will provide the applicable approval process and timelines.

Joining and leaving a project
New team members should complete the onboarding steps and request only the access needed for their work. When responsibilities change or employment ends, notify the manager and HR so company access and property can be reviewed.

Questions about this sample
Ask HR or the organization administrator for current procedures, official terms, and the correct contact details. Replace this sample with the organization's approved handbook before relying on it.
