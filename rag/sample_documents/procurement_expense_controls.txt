SAMPLE INTERNAL POLICY — Procurement and Expense Controls
This is an illustrative company template, not a statement of legal or regulatory requirements.

Purchases and expenses
- Obtain approval from the authorized budget owner before making a non-routine commitment on the company's behalf.
- Keep the business purpose, amount, date, vendor, and itemized receipt with each expense claim.
- Do not approve your own expense claim or split a purchase to avoid an approval limit.
- Finance reviews claims for completeness and routes exceptions to the appropriate approver.

Vendor and payment handling
Use the approved vendor onboarding and payment-change process. Independently verify unusual or changed payment instructions using a known contact method before acting. Restrict invoices, bank details, and payment records to authorized staff.

Questions about this sample
The company owner must adapt this template to actual contracts, approval limits, and applicable law before adopting it.
