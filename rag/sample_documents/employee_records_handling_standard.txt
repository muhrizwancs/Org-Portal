SAMPLE INTERNAL POLICY — Employee Records Handling
This is an illustrative company template, not a statement of legal or regulatory requirements.

Access and storage
- HR records are confidential and are limited to authorized HR staff and other specifically approved personnel.
- Keep records in approved, access-controlled company systems. Do not send employee records to personal email or store them on unmanaged devices.
- Verify the recipient and business purpose before sharing a record. Share only the minimum information required.

Retention and requests
Follow the company-approved retention schedule for personnel files and securely dispose of records when retention ends. Send employee requests about their records to the designated HR contact and record how the request was handled.

Questions about this sample
The company owner must adapt this template to actual records, contracts, and applicable law before adopting it.
